The Thesis

Distance from the city is not just a coordinate. It is a destiny.

Across the European Union, regions classified as "predominantly rural" consistently report lower employment, shorter lives, and fewer university graduates than their urban counterparts. These gaps are not random — they follow the routes of railways and motorways, or rather, the absence of them.

The 15-minute city — a planning concept promising every amenity within a quarter-hour's walk or cycle — has become a touchstone of progressive urbanism. But for hundreds of millions of Europeans, the nearest hospital, job centre, or university is not 15 minutes away. It is a car journey that many cannot afford, or a bus that runs twice a day.

The Evidence

Transport infrastructure and life outcomes move together.

Using eight Eurostat datasets covering {{ stats.n_regions }} NUTS-2 regions across all 27 EU member states, this project maps four outcome domains — employment, education, health, and transport connectivity — and shows how they co-vary with the urban-rural classification of each region.

The pattern is stark. Regions with higher railway and motorway density cluster at the top of every outcome ranking. The correlation is not merely about wealth: even within countries, better-connected regions outperform their more isolated neighbours on every indicator.

"A region without trains is a region without a future." — Paraphrasing the 2023 Eurostat Regional Yearbook
The Stakes

Cohesion funding alone cannot bridge the divide.

The EU spends over €350 billion per programming period on cohesion policy, much of it on infrastructure. Yet the urban-rural gap persists, and in some indicators is widening. The question this project poses is not whether money is being spent, but whether the right connections are being built — and whether the regions that most need them are receiving them.

The data pages that follow do not prescribe solutions. They tell you what the numbers say: that where you can go in 15 minutes shapes almost everything else about your life.
